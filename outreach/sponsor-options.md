# Commons sponsor options

**For:** Steering Committee members-designate (SNGR, INAMHI), the PM and operator management. **Question:** who should sponsor the P2 Commons (`ectwin-commons-prod`), and how? **Status:** proposal, 2026-09-30.

Figures come from the planning documents (USD, before IVA/ISD); anything else is marked **(unverified)** or **(to confirm)**, and weeks to first money are estimates (est.).

## 1. Recommendation

Name **SNGR** the Commons sponsor of record: it signs the term sheet by 2026-10-09, commits the **US$305,311** Phase 0–1 bridge (floor **US$223,776**) and Commons funding through **2027-04-30**, and holds the Commons billing account from December 2026. Fund the peak with a non-reimbursable technical-cooperation grant from **CAF**, with **IDB** approached in parallel (both to confirm), and until SNGR can pay, let the **operator in kind** (if its management agrees) or else **CEDIA** hold a capped bridge billing account of ≈US$900 of cloud to 2026-11-27. The cloud ask is small (a Commons invoice of ≈US$72–97 in the pilot month, ≈US$273–356 in a season month and ≈US$444–602 in an event month, plus a ≈US$446/month T4 pool), and from October 2027 the Commons passes to an SNGR–INAMHI host (H1) under clause 13.

### 4.4 How billing and roles transfer

1. **Bridge to SNGR.** Link each `ectwin-commons-*` and T4 project to SNGR's billing account (Billing Account User on the account, Project Billing Manager on the project, as in [04 §10.1](../docs/04-identity-tenancy-byo-gcp.md#101-scenarios)); recreate budgets and billing export; close the bridge account. Project IDs do not change, so the Flood API allowlist and archive stay in place (to confirm with Google).
2. **Operator to host.** Clause 13 (*traspaso institucional*) passes the operator's *convenio* rights and obligations to «la entidad pública o consorcio que asuma la operación de la plataforma, previa notificación escrita, sin necesidad de suscribir un nuevo convenio» ([13 §5.3](../docs/13-governance-legal-risk.md#53-template-clauses-spanish-proposals-pending-lc-review)). PT sends the notices, the DPO novates the DPAs ([12 §10.3](../docs/12-roadmap-team-budget.md#103-transfer-checklist)), and the term sheet mirrors clause 13 for the sponsor's rights.
3. **Projects and licences.** If SNGR hosts, billing does not move again; the projects move with a 3-month parallel run. WeatherNext approvals are non-transferable, so the host applies itself or Google agrees in writing first ([13 §9.4](../docs/13-governance-legal-risk.md#94-phase-4-institutional-hand-over)).

### 5.3 Term sheet contents

1. **Parties and roles:** sponsor of record, co-funders, operator, host-designate, and the sponsor's SC seat.
2. **Scope:** `ectwin-commons-{dev,stg,prod}` with Block D, national Jev and Gemini, and the T4 pool; C8 optional.
3. **Amounts and tranches:** the bridge (US$305,311; floor US$223,776) on signature, Phase 2 at G1b, Phase 3 at G2; Commons budgets US$450/US$650; T4 US$650/month.
4. **Term:** through at least 2027-04-30, renewable to 2027-09-30. At least 3 months must always be committed, with 90 days' notice (as in *convenio* clause 15).
5. **Billing:** the holder of `<BA_SPONSOR>` at each stage and the switch date; route R-A or R-E; billing export; no operator re-invoicing (LP-10).
6. **Licences:** Commons WeatherNext licensee and Flood API project; operator as Contractor; Non-Retrievable Value-Added products only.
7. **Neutrality:** no sponsor role in *umbrales*, verification or wording; D1; the SC chair decides co-branding; a conflict-of-interest declaration.
8. **Reporting:** monthly report by the 10th; alerts at 50/90/100%; SC approval for reallocations above 10% and contingency draws above US$10,000.
9. **Continuity:** degraded mode if funding lapses (daily cadence, archive kept); 60-day notice to T4 tenants; the N2+ top-up.
10. **Transfer:** mirror of clause 13; project and billing moves; code (Apache-2.0) and archive stay open.
11. **Tax:** IVA and ISD per the GOV-M5 memo (2026-11-06); B5.
12. **Conditions precedent and signatures.**
